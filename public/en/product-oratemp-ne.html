Description

Non-eugenol temporary cement for placement and retention of permanent crowns, bridges, splints and their temporary counterparts; insoluble in oral fluids.

Indications

Cementation of crowns, bridges and splints · Retention of temporary crowns and bridges · Trial cementation of permanent restorations before final insertion

Benefits

Excellent physical properties · Easy to mix, easy to clean · Low solubility in oral fluids

Packaging

Manual Mix: 1 × 15 g cartridge, 1 × spatula, 1 × mixing pad (REF 11003). Auto Mix: 1 × 15 g automix cartridge, 20 × mixing tips (REF 11004).

Storage

Store at 10–24 °C, away from direct sunlight and moisture. Follow the instructions for use.
