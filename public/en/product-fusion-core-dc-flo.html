Fusion Core DC Flo

Dual-cure core build-up composite · Prevest DenPro · Art. no. AM-D-107
Price on request – the ANMA Medicals Team will contact you with an offer

Flowable, dual-cure, nano-tech core build-up composite in a convenient auto-mix syringe.

  • For professional use by dentists and healthcare professionals only
  • CE-marked medical device · manufacturer: Prevest DenPro Limited
Description

Dual-cure, two-component core build-up material in an auto-mix delivery syringe. Its flow stacks easily without slumping for good placement, and the dual-cure formulation facilitates complete curing.

Indications

Core build-up for vital and non-vital teeth · luting of fibre-reinforced resin posts.

Benefits

Light curing for time-saving application · additional chemical curing for complete polymerisation · low heat development even in bulk application · flowable, non-slumping formula for convenient placement · low shrinkage.

Packaging

1 × 9 g automix syringe, 10 × intra-oral tips, 10 × endo tips, 1 × instruction manual.

Storage & contraindications

Contains methacrylates, amines, peroxides and BHT: do not use in case of known hypersensitivity to any of these ingredients. Avoid zinc oxide eugenol materials. Store at 10–24 °C, closed, away from moisture. Shelf life 2 years from date of manufacture.
