VPS heavy body impression material · Prevest DenPro · Art. no. AM-D-172

  • CE-marked medical device · manufacturer: Prevest DenPro Limited
Description

Heavy-body polyvinyl siloxane impression material for heavy wash and double-arch impressions.

Indications

Impressions for indirect restorations (crowns, bridges, inlays, onlays, veneers) · Dental implant impressions · Edentulous impressions and bite registrations

Benefits

Accurate reproduction of details · Adequate working and setting time · Excellent dimensional stability

Packaging

Regular Set: 2 × 50 ml base/catalyst paste, 10 × mixing tips (REF 70015). Regular Set: 1 × 50 ml base/catalyst paste, 5 × mixing tips (REF 70015-1). Fast Set: 1 × 50 ml base/catalyst paste, 5 × mixing tips (REF 70015-2). Fast Set: 2 × 50 ml base/catalyst paste, 10 × mixing tips (REF 70015-3).

Storage

Store at 10–24 °C, away from direct sunlight and moisture. Follow the instructions for use.
