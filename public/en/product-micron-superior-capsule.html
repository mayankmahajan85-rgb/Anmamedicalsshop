Micron Superior Capsule

Glass ionomer cement · Prevest DenPro · Art. no. AM-D-104
Price on request – the ANMA Medicals Team will contact you with an offer

Aesthetic fine-particle glass ionomer restorative cement in prefilled capsules.

  • CE-marked medical device · manufacturer: Prevest DenPro Limited
Description

Type 2 restorative glass ionomer cement in prefilled capsules for restoration of primary and permanent teeth, with sustained fluoride release and high compressive strength.

Indications

Restoration of primary teeth · core build-up · restoration of class III, V and limited class I cavities.

Benefits

Optimised powder/liquid ratio · high wear resistance · simple to place and easy to handle · self-curing/self-adherent · radiopaque with strong adhesion to enamel and dentine · high and sustained fluoride release · coefficient of expansion similar to tooth structure.

Packaging

10 single-shade capsules and 1 instruction manual. A capsule mixer and capsule applier are required for processing.

Storage & contraindications

Store at 10–24 °C, away from direct sunlight and moisture. Shelf life 3 years from date of manufacture. Not for pulp capping.
