Description

Hydrophilic vinyl polysiloxane impression kit with putty and light body for the dual-phase impression technique.

Indications

Dual-phase impression technique with putty and light body · Impressions of dentulous and edentulous arches and implants · Precise duplication of models

Benefits

Accurate and extremely smooth impressions · Hydrophilic with exceptional dimensional stability · High elasticity and tear resistance

Packaging

Combo Kit: 1 × 150 ml putty base paste, 1 × 150 ml putty catalyst paste, 1 × 50 ml light body base/catalyst paste, 5 × mixing tips, 2 × spoons (REF 70004-1).

Storage

Store at 10–24 °C, away from direct sunlight and moisture. Follow the instructions for use.
