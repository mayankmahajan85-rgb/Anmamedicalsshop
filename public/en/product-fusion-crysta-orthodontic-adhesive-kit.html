Orthodontic bonding kit · Prevest DenPro · Art. no. AM-D-119

Description

Light-cure composite kit for bonding metal and ceramic brackets and cementing molar bands.

Indications

Bonding metal and ceramic brackets to the tooth surface · Cementation of molar bands

Benefits

Highly translucent adhesive · Primer with 10-MDP for physical and chemical adhesion · High enamel bond strength

Packaging

MDP Orthodontic Kit: 1 × 4 g syringe, 1 × 5 ml bottle, 1 × 5 ml syringe, 10 × applicator tips (REF 10024). Regular Orthodontic Kit: 1 × 4 g syringe, 1 × 5 ml bottle, 1 × 5 ml syringe, 10 × applicator tips (REF 10024-1).

Storage

Store at 10–24 °C, away from direct sunlight and moisture. Follow the instructions for use.
