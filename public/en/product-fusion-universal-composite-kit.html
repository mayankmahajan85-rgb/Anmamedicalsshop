Fusion Universal Composite Kit

Nano-hybrid composite · Prevest DenPro · Art. no. AM-D-101
Price on request – the ANMA Medicals Team will contact you with an offer

Light-cure nano-hybrid universal restorative composite for all cavity classes in the anterior and posterior region.

  • Price and delivery time are provided in your personal quotation
  • The ANMA Medicals Team will contact you

Description

Radiopaque, highly filled, fine hybrid composite. Universal restorative composite for all cavity classes of the anterior and posterior region, with lower polymerisation shrinkage than conventional composites and extremely high abrasion resistance.

Indications

Direct restoration of all cavity classes in anterior and posterior teeth · preventive resin restoration and repair of composite and ceramic veneers · aesthetic corrections · core build-up and composite inlays.

Benefits

Excellent physical properties for long-lasting restorations · lower polymerisation shrinkage · high filler content · extremely high abrasion resistance.

Packaging

Intro Kit: 4 × 4 g syringes (A2, A3, B2, B3), 1 × 5 ml Fusion Bond 5, 1 × 2 ml Actino Gel, 10 × micro brushes, 10 × applicator tips, instruction manual. Combo Kit: 7 × 4 g syringes (A1, A2, A3, A3.5, B1, B2, B3), 1 × 5 ml Fusion Bond 5, 1 × 2 ml Actino etching gel, 10 × micro brushes, 10 × applicator tips, instruction manual.

Storage & contraindications

Store at 10–24 °C, away from direct sunlight and moisture. Shelf life 3 years from date of manufacture. Do not use in patients allergic to methacrylate resins.
