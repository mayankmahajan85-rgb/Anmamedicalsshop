ACE Repair Kit

Ceramic repair kit · Prevest DenPro · Art. no. AM-D-106
Price on request – the ANMA Medicals Team will contact you with an offer

Chair-side ceramic repair kit for intraoral repairs of ceramic and composite restorations.

1
  • Price and delivery time are provided in your personal quotation
  • The ANMA Medicals Team will contact you
  • For professional use by dentists and healthcare professionals only
Description

Comprehensive set for intraoral repairs of ceramic and composite restorations. The kit comprises the necessary bonding and veneering materials: Silane-X (silanating agent), CeraEtch (5% hydrofluoric acid), Renew Universal (10-MDP + 4-META bonding agent) and Fusion Flo (flowable composite).

Indications

Repair of porcelain-fused-to-metal crowns and bridges, inlays, onlays and all-ceramic restorations.

Packaging

1 × 1.2 ml syringe CeraEtch + 5 applicator tips, 1 × 1.2 ml syringe Silane-X + 5 applicator tips, 1 × 1.2 ml syringe Fusion Flo Opaque + 5 applicator tips, 1 × 1.2 ml syringe Renew Universal + 5 applicator tips, 1 × instruction manual.

Warnings & storage

Read the instructions before use. CeraEtch contains hydrofluoric acid, a very powerful chemical: handle with extreme care. Silane-X contains flammable solvents; keep away from ignition sources. Store at 10–24 °C, away from moisture. Shelf life 2 years from date of manufacture.
