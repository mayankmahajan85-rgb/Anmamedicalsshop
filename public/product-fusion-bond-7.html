Description

Fusion Bond 7 is a light-cure, one-component dentine/enamel bonding agent for both direct and indirect restorations. It primes and bonds in one step.

Indications

Adhesion of composite resin for direct and indirect restorations.

Benefits

Single-step self-etch bonding agent for direct composite restorations · excellent adhesion to dentine and enamel · long-lasting bond strength · universal for all light-curing restorations.

Packaging

Intro Pack: 1 × 5 ml squeeze bottle, instruction manual. Economy Pack: 2 × 5 ml squeeze bottles, 40 × micro brushes, instruction manual.

Warnings

Irritant. Do not use if the patient is allergic to any of the ingredients. Do not use for direct pulp capping. Store at 10–24 °C, away from direct sunlight and moisture. Shelf life 3 years from date of manufacture.
